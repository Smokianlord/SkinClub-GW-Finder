Copy confirmation animation

- Clicking the inline LINK copy icon shows a compact "LINK COPIED" pill at the click location.
- Clicking the PROMOCODE copy icon shows a compact "CODE COPIED" pill at the click location.
- The confirmation fades/slides in, remains readable briefly, then floats upward and fades out.
- It never steals focus and does not replace Deep Search / Refresh progress text.
- Repeated copy clicks replace the previous confirmation cleanly.
